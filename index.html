(01) Fauves

The Fauves were painters who set colour free. A century later, the people who live most wildly — who build, lead, carry, decide — are the ones with the least room to breathe.

Fauves began with one of them. A client who worked without pause, whose walls were full of things chosen to impress. I didn’t paint him what he owned. I painted him what he’d lost: a place to rest.

Every work begins with conversation. Then it is made in soft pastel — the purest, most delicate pigment there is, the medium museums keep in the dark to protect.

Each sanctuary is made for one person, one house, or one place, and never repeated.

(03) The Sanctuary Commission

By private inquiry

Not a picture of what you own. A portrait of what you stand for — the calm, the values, the feeling a place should hold.

For Private ClientsA sanctuary for one person: the place they return to when they finally exhale. For a home, a study, a bedroom, a yacht.
For Companies & FoundersA work that holds what a company stands for: its values, its origin, its people. For a headquarters, a boardroom, a founder’s office.
For Hotels & InteriorsWorks or series made for a place and the feeling it should give its guests. For hotels, residences and members’ clubs, in collaboration with interior designers and architects.
  1. (i) The ConversationPrivate sessions with you, your team, or your designer. What you carry, what you value, what the work should make people feel.
  2. (ii) The Colour StudyA small study and the story behind it, presented to you privately before the work begins.
  3. (iii) The WorkMade by hand in the studio, in soft pastel on archival paper. Unhurried, and for you alone.
  4. (iv) The DeliverySealed in conservation framing, hand-delivered, with a letter on what was seen and how it became the work.
